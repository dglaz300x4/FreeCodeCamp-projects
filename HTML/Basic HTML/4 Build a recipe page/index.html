Banana Chocolate Chip Pancake Recipe

Welcome to my recipe on how to make the most amazing banana and chocolate chip pancakes! This recipe will really boost your cooking skills and make you an amazing breakfast chef. I am not a fantastic cook, but I love to make breakfast for my girlfriend and I, and this is a recipe we both love!

Ingredients

  • 1 Ripe Banana
  • 3/4 Cup of semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • 1 1/2 Cups of Great Value Pancake Mix
  • 1 1/3 Cups of Water
  • 1 teaspoon of Vanilla Extract
  • 1 teaspoon of Ground Cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Mash banana with a fork and set it off to the side
  2. Mix your Cinnamon in with your pancake mix
  3. Pour and mix your water and vanilla extract in with your pancake and cinnamon mix until you get a semi-thick, but runny batter
  4. Mix your mashed banana in with your batter until you cant see many of the banana chuncks
  5. Now pour and mix your chocolate chips into your batter
  6. For each pancake:
    1. Pour your pancake batter into your desired pancake size
    2. Let it cook until the bubbles start to pop
    3. Flip pancake and let it cook for about 1 1/2 minutes
