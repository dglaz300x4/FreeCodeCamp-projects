Breaking: AI bubble bursts

Companies Rush To Hire Employees As The AI bubble Burst

BY Jordan Glazier

Many companies in the last few years have been heavily investing into AI (Artificial Intelligence) and AI related companies, and with many gaining success in their investments. Companies like Open AI have gained large amounts of success for their efforts in developing open source AI models and their product ChatGPT. Even companies such as Microsoft have gone as far as including their own AI Co-pilot into their operating systems. But in a sudden turn of events yesterday, that bubble has burst.

Yesterday, many of those companies that have invested recieved news that their invetments would not be making a large return. And in some cases, many of those invesments have been lost. Companies such as Open AI have made the announcement that while many people are using AI in their daily lives, the costs have become large than the income they are earning. This news has lead to mass layoffs from the companies, and a lack of trust from their investors. Companies such as Walmart have began to back away from the use of AI in their products and apps, and began to hire more people to get the job done that AI wont be able to do now.
