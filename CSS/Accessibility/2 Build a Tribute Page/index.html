Peter Cullen

The voice of generations

  • 1941 - Born in Montreal, Quebec, Canada
  • 1963 - Graduates from the National Theatre School in Canada
  • 1964 - Entered his first television role in "Action Theatre"
  • 1966 - Was the host of the "Milkman's Maintee" overnight program on the radio station CKGM
  • 1966 - Landed his first reoccuring role in a television series: Johnny Jellybean and Friends
  • 1967 - Played "Commander Bi Bi Latuque"
  • 1971 - 1975 - An announcer and performer on The Sonny and Cher Comedy Hour
  • 1983 - Peter is offered the "opportunity of a lifetime" as the role of Optimus Prime in the original G1 Transformers series, and his brother Larry Cullen gives him the advice of "If you're going to be a hero, be a real hero. Be strong enough to be gentle. Be compassionate, understanding, and be true."
  • 1984 - 1986 - Peter is the voice of Optimus Prime in Transformers until the characters death in the 1986 movie, only appearing briefly in a couple of episodes in the later seasons
  • 2007 - Peter reprised his role as Optimus Prime in the the 2007 Transformers movie by Michael Bay, and all of its live action sequels
  • 2011 - Cullen is nomiated for a 2011 Daytime Emmy Award in the Outstanding Performer in an Animated Program for his voice work in Transformers: Prime
  • 2023 - Cullen recieved the lifetime acheivement award at the 2023 Children's and Family Emmy Awards
  • 2026 - Passed away at the age of 85
